Welcome to my little corner of the internet. This site is built entirely with Rust, featuring real-time ASCII art rendering and a retro terminal aesthetic.

## Why ASCII?

There's something beautiful about constraints. When you limit yourself to monospace characters and box-drawing symbols, you're forced to be creative. Plus, it loads fast and works everywhere.

- **Projects** - Things I'm building and breaking
- **Tutorials** - Sharing what I learn
- **Random thoughts** - Brain dumps and ideas
